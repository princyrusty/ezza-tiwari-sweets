Our Story

From the oldest sweet shop of Champawat

For generations, Tiwari Sweets has been a name the people of Champawat trust, known as the town's oldest sweet shop. In the lanes of this Kumaoni hill town, sweets are part of every celebration, every festival and every homecoming.

Ezza is our new venture, created to share three of our most loved sweets with families across India: wholesome Mandua sweets, the iconic Bal Mithai and our rich Chocolate Barfi. Same recipes, same purity, now at your doorstep.

— The Tiwari Family, Champawat
